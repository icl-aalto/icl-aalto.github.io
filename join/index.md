# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-handshake" %}Join Us

<p class="lead lead-wide">
We are always looking for <strong>outstanding talent</strong>: open-minded, collaborative people with strong mathematical
and programming skills who want to work on the foundations of machine learning and AI.
</p>

We welcome applications for:

- **PhD students**
- **Postdocs**
- **Visiting PhD students**, e.g. through mobility programmes
- **Research assistants** at Aalto University

The lab is part of the [Department of Computer Science at Aalto University](https://research.aalto.fi/en/organisations/professorship-chen-qi)
and the [ELLIS Institute Finland](https://www.ellisinstitute.fi/).
