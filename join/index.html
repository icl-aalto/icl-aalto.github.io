Join Us

We are always looking for outstanding talent: open-minded, collaborative people with strong mathematical and programming skills who want to work on the foundations of machine learning and AI.

We welcome applications for:

  • PhD students
  • Postdocs
  • Visiting PhD students, e.g. through mobility programmes
  • Research assistants at Aalto University

The lab is part of the Department of Computer Science at Aalto University and the ELLIS Institute Finland.

Interested? Start by filling out our expression of interest form, whichever position you have in mind, including if you plan to apply through one of the calls below.

HIIT Postdoctoral Fellow Positions

Deadline: 4 October 2026, 23:59 (UTC+3)

The Helsinki Institute for Information Technology (HIIT) is hiring Postdoctoral Fellows in Computer Science at Aalto University and the University of Helsinki. Qi Chen is one of the listed supervisors (generative models, continual learning, learning theory).

  • Duration: up to three years, flexible start date; fully funded, including salary and travel/publication costs
  • Eligibility: a PhD (or soon) in computer science, statistics, mathematics or a related field, with a strong publication record

  • Application: cover letter (1–2 pages), preliminary research plan (3–5 pages), CV with publication list, doctoral transcript and degree certificate, and contact details of 2–3 referees. If you would like to work with us, say so and explain your motivation in the cover letter.
