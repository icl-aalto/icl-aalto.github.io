Qi Chen is a Principal Investigator at the [ELLIS Institute Finland](https://www.ellisinstitute.fi/) and a Tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at [Aalto University](https://research.aalto.fi/en/organisations/professorship-chen-qi), and a member of ELLIS.

Before this, Qi was a postdoctoral fellow at the Data Science Institute and the [Robot Vision & Learning Lab](https://rvl.cs.toronto.edu/) at the University of Toronto, supported by the [DSI Postdoc Fellowship](https://datasciences.utoronto.ca/postdoctoral-fellows/) and advised by Prof. [Florian Shkurti](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~florian/), and a Faculty Affiliate Researcher at the [Vector Institute](https://vectorinstitute.ai/).
Qi received a Ph.D. (dissertation with honor) from Laval University in 2024, supervised by Prof. [Mario Marchand](https://scholar.google.ca/citations?user=M792u2sAAAAJ&hl=en).
Earlier, Qi was a senior algorithm engineer at Baidu and ByteDance, and studied at [Télécom Paris, Institut Polytechnique de Paris](https://www.telecom-paris.fr/en/home) (diplôme d'ingénieur) and [Chien-Shiung Wu College, Southeast University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeast_University) (B.Eng.).

Service: Main organizer of the ELLIS Summer School [AI4Research 2026](https://www.ellisinstitute.fi/ellis-summer-school-2026); Action Editor for TMLR; Area Chair for ICLR 2027; reviewer for NeurIPS (Outstanding Reviewer 2022), ICML (Silver Reviewer 2026), ICLR, AISTATS, AAAI, TPAMI and TNNLS.
